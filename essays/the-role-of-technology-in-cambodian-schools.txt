The Role of Technology in Cambodian Schools

Technology is becoming increasingly important in education, including in Cambodian schools. Digital tools can provide students and teachers with new ways to access information, communicate, and learn. However, schools need to use technology appropriately and address differences in access. In my opinion, technology can benefit Cambodian education by improving learning resources, supporting teachers, and developing students' digital skills.
First, technology can give Cambodian students greater access to learning resources. Students can use educational websites, videos, digital books, and online platforms to study subjects beyond the materials available in their schools. For example, a student in a rural area can use an educational video to review a science topic that was difficult to understand in class. This can provide additional learning opportunities.
Second, technology can support teachers and improve lesson delivery. Teachers can use presentations, videos, digital exercises, and online assessment tools to make lessons more varied and interactive. For instance, an English teacher can use audio recordings and videos to give students additional listening and pronunciation practice. Technology can therefore complement traditional teaching methods.
Third, technology can help students develop digital skills that are important for their future. Students need to know how to search for information, communicate online, create digital content, and use technology responsibly. For example, teachers can ask students to research a topic online and evaluate whether their sources are reliable. This develops both digital literacy and critical thinking.
In conclusion, technology can play a valuable role in Cambodian schools. It can increase access to learning resources, support teachers, and prepare students for a digital future. However, schools should also work to reduce unequal access to devices and Internet services. When used appropriately and responsibly, technology can contribute to better education in Cambodia.
