How to Improve Students' Academic Performance

Improving students' academic performance is an important goal for schools because education should help students develop knowledge and skills effectively. Academic performance can be affected by teaching quality, motivation, study habits, family support, and learning conditions. In my opinion, schools can improve student performance by using effective teaching, providing support, and encouraging students to take responsibility for learning.
First, teachers should use effective and appropriate teaching methods. Students have different learning needs, so teachers should combine explanations with activities, practice, questions, and examples. For example, after explaining a new concept, a teacher can provide exercises and group activities that allow students to apply what they have learned.
Second, students need regular feedback and additional support. Teachers can identify areas of difficulty through classroom observation and assessment. Students who are struggling can receive extra practice, individual guidance, or peer support. For instance, a student who has difficulty reading can receive additional reading practice and feedback instead of simply receiving a low grade.
Third, students and parents should contribute to academic success. Students need good study habits, regular attendance, time management, and realistic goals. Parents can provide encouragement and a suitable environment for studying at home. For example, parents can help students establish a regular study schedule without doing the work for them.
In conclusion, improving academic performance requires cooperation among teachers, students, parents, and schools. Effective teaching, appropriate support, good study habits, and positive learning environments can all contribute to better results. Schools should focus not only on examination scores but also on helping students develop strong learning habits and useful skills.
